## Known Limitations

- kicad-cli works in file mode only; it does not change a design open in a running KiCad (KiCad 10.0.6 and later).
- `footprint move` moves and turns unlocked footprints but does not flip them to the other side, and tracks do not follow; it does not move or turn a footprint that holds a zone, a text box or a dimension (kicad-cli 1.1.0, all KiCad versions).
- `track create` draws straight segments only, no arcs; `via create` adds through vias only, no blind, buried or micro vias. `track delete` refuses arcs and locked segments (kicad-cli 1.1.0).
- `zone create` writes the outline with KiCad's default zone settings and no fill; the file holds a fill only after KiCad refills the zones and saves (kicad-cli 1.1.0).
- `board update` sets only rectangular outlines and replaces only an outline that is one rectangle, or none (kicad-cli 1.1.0).
- The board writes refuse boards saved by KiCad 9 or earlier; open and save them once in KiCad 10 (kicad-cli 1.1.0).
- `symbol move` refuses multi-unit symbols and symbols wired directly to another part; new sheets and reused sheets cannot be created (kicad-cli 1.1.0).
- Checks get 25 seconds and exports 40 seconds; KiCad's DRC on a large board can take longer (kicad-cli 1.1.0, KiCad 10.0.6 and later).
- A Flatpak KiCad on Linux is not found (kicad-cli 1.1.0).
